Key Finding:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  For T_Co with δ = 1, all diagonal entries of the Gram matrix are even.
  Therefore, div(v) = gcd(v·w : w ∈ T) is always EVEN for any v ∈ T_Co.
  All primitive isotropic vectors have div(v) = 2.
  There are NO primitive isotropic vectors with div(v) = 1.
