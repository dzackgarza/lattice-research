# Central Literature Spine

This file is the canonical reference map for standard claims used throughout the repo.
Use it before adding new derivations or background prose.

## Core claim families

### Coble curve, blowup model, and K3 cover

- **A. B. Coble (1917)**: *The ten nodes of the rational plane sextic*. American Journal
  of Mathematics, 39(2), 327–372.
  - Use for: the classical 10-nodal rational sextic and the basic blowup construction.
- **A. B. Coble (1929)**: *Algebraic Geometry and Theta Functions*. American
  Mathematical Society.
  - Use for: classical background on the point-configuration geometry surrounding Coble
    surfaces and curves.

### Coble / nodal Enriques moduli and the period-domain picture

- **I. Dolgachev & S. Kondō (2013)**: *The rationality of the moduli spaces of Coble
  surfaces and of nodal Enriques surfaces*.
  - Use for: the moduli-theoretic Coble/Enriques picture, rationality statements, and
    the standard period-domain framing that the repo should cite before attempting any
    local restatement of the 9-dimensional claim.
- **H. Sterk (1991)**: *Compactifications of the period space of Enriques surfaces.
  I*.
  - Use for: isotropic orbit lifting, period-space boundary structure, and the Enriques
    side of the period-domain machinery.
- **F. Scattone (1987)**: *On the Compactification of Moduli Spaces for Algebraic K3
  Surfaces*.
  - Use for: Type IV period-domain / Baily–Borel boundary background on the K3 side.

### Lattice-theoretic structure and embeddings

- **V. Nikulin (1979)**: *Integer symmetric bilinear forms and some of their geometric
  applications*.
  - Use for: 2-elementary lattices, primitive embeddings, discriminant-form arguments,
    and uniqueness/surjectivity criteria.
- **V. Nikulin (1980)**: *Factor groups of the automorphism groups of hyperbolic forms
  by subgroup generated by 2-reflections*.
  - Use for: reflection-group background when the repo discusses arithmetic groups and
    reflection-generated structures.

### Modern compactification / KSBA layer

- **V. Alexeev, P. Engel, D. Zack Garza, & M. Schaffler (2023)**: *Compact moduli of
  Enriques surfaces with a numerical polarization of degree 2*.
  - Use for: the modern Enriques compactification layer, IAS language, and nodal K3
    cover context.
- **D. Zack Garza (2024)**: *Complete Moduli Spaces of Coble Surfaces*.
  - Use for: the project's own Coble-surface compactification narrative.
- **Daniel Zachary Garza (2026)**: *Compact Moduli of Numerically Polarized Degree 2
  Enriques Surfaces*.
  - Use for: dissertation-level details on the degree-2 Enriques side.

## How to use this file

- If a claim is standard background, cite one of the sources above before writing local
  justification.
- If a computation is meant to support a standard fact, say explicitly that it is
  *supporting evidence* or an *exact worked example*, not the primary source.
- If a claim is not obviously covered here, add it to this spine before building more
  repo prose around it.
