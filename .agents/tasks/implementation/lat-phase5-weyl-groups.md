## Source-Grounded Contract

Source anchors: `pln-lattice-phase-5-orthogonal-groups.md` (Step 5.6),
`category_specs/lattices/docs/MAPPING.md`,
`lattice-interface-style-guide.md`,
`theory/backends/vinberg-algorithm.md`, and `theory/references/index.md`.

- `W(L)` is the subgroup of `O(L)` generated by reflections in roots of `L`.
- `weyl_group()` / `W()` return a subgroup object of the orthogonal group whose
  generators are reflection morphisms; membership and word operations are group-level
  semantics, not lattice helpers.
- `simple_reflections()` and `coxeter_diagram()` are allowed only when the simple-root
  data is actually supplied by the chosen backend or by a finite crystallographic
  constructor. This card owns the subgroup object and reflection-generated semantics,
  not the global search for a simple-root basis.
- `is_reflection`, `reflection_decomposition`, and related word methods belong on the
  morphism/group layer and must return morphisms or words in declared generators rather
  than raw matrices.

Backend routing:
- Finite crystallographic lattices may use Sage/GAP root-system data where already
  available.
- Hyperbolic or indefinite simple-root / Coxeter generation routes to the Vinberg
  backend family; the local Weyl-group layer consumes those roots and reflections but
  does not hand-roll a replacement algorithm.
