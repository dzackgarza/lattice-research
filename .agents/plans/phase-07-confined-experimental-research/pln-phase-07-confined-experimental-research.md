# Phase 07 confined experimental Coble research

## Objective

Find publishable Coble moduli results inside the semantic mathematical language built by
the previous phases. Results must be reviewable as mathematical chains of argument, not
as opaque raw computations. Mature claims should be transitioned toward paper prose and,
where feasible, formalized proof artifacts.

## Lean and Aristotle thread

Formalization becomes a first-class output in this phase. The correct route is still
incremental: assemble the small definitions and lemmas formalized in earlier phases,
fill missing bridge lemmas, then attempt larger theorem statements only when the paper
argument is mathematically stable.

Aristotle can run asynchronously for bounded proof-search and formalization tasks. It is
not a substitute for source-backed statements, semantic repo code, or human mathematical
review.

## Entry criteria

- [ ] Category, lattice, and geometry interfaces are stable, source-backed, and tested.
- [ ] Known primary-source results are recovered through the semantic layer.
- [ ] Phase-transition QC has passed for the committed implementation surface.
- [ ] Earlier Lean vocabulary and lemma cards are available for the target research direction.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] Experimental findings are expressed through semantic objects and vetted algorithms.
- [ ] Promising results are ready to be translated into paper-level mathematical prose.
- [ ] Mature proof chains have corresponding Lean formalization plans or checked formal artifacts where feasible.
