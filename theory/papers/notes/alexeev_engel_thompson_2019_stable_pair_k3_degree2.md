## BB boundary of F₂ (degree-2 K3s): explicit structure for test fixtures

**Source:** Alexeev-Engel-Thompson 2019 (arXiv:1903.09742), Section 3–4. Originally due to Scattone 1987.

### Setup

- **K3 lattice:** Λ_K3 ≅ H³ ⊕ E₈² of signature (3,19)
- **Polarization:** primitive h ∈ Λ_K3 with h² = 2
- **Period lattice:** L := h^⊥ ≅ H² ⊕ E₈² ⊕ ⟨-2⟩, signature (2,19)
- **Period domain:** D = connected component of {x ∈ L⊗ℂ : x·x̄ > 0, x·x = 0} ⊂ P(L⊗ℂ), associated to O⁺(2,19)
- **Arithmetic group:** Γ = Γ₂ = spinor-norm-1 subgroup of Stab(h) ⊂ O(Λ_K3)
- **Moduli space:** F₂ = Γ\D, dim = 19

### BB boundary structure

∂F₂^BB consists of:
- **One 0-cusp** (Type III point): unique Γ-orbit of primitive isotropic lines I ⊂ L
- **Four 1-cusps** (Type II curves): four distinct Γ-orbits of primitive isotropic planes J ⊂ L

The four 1-cusps are labeled by the root sublattice of finite index in J^⊥/J:

| Cusp label | Root sublattice in J^⊥/J |
|---|---|
| Ã₁₇ | A₁₇ |
| D̃₁₀Ẽ₇ | D₁₀ ⊕ E₇ |
| Ẽ₈²Ã₁ | E₈ ⊕ E₈ ⊕ A₁ |
| D̃₁₆Ã₁ | D₁₆ ⊕ A₁ |

These are exactly the four maximal parabolic subdiagrams of the Coxeter diagram G_cox (mod S₃ symmetry).

### The 0-cusp: isotropic vector and quotient lattice

For the unique 0-cusp, take any primitive isotropic v ∈ L with v² = 0.
- **Quotient lattice:** N := v^⊥/v ≅ H ⊕ E₈² ⊕ A₁, signature (1,18)
- **Stabilizer:** Γ_v = Stab_Γ(v)/U_v where U_v is the unipotent (translation) subgroup
- **Acts as:** Γ_v ≅ O⁺(N) (index-2 subgroup of O(N) preserving positive cone)

### Coxeter diagram and fan

N = H ⊕ E₈² ⊕ A₁ is a hyperbolic root lattice. Its Weyl group W(N) (generated by reflections in roots r ∈ N with r² = -2) has finite index in O⁺(N), with quotient O⁺(N)/W(N) ≅ S₃.

**Coxeter diagram G_cox:** 24 nodes r₀,...,r₂₃ (roots generating N), with edges:
- no edge: (rᵢ,rⱼ) = 0
- single edge: (rᵢ,rⱼ) = 1
- double edge: (rᵢ,rⱼ) = 2
- dashed edge: (rᵢ,rⱼ) = 6

**Fundamental chamber 𝔎** of W(N): {x ∈ C̄ : (x,rᵢ) ≥ 0 for all i}

**Coxeter fan 𝔉_cox:** walls = perpendiculars of roots (r² = -2 vectors). Cones mod O⁺(N) ↔ elliptic and maximal parabolic subdiagrams of G_cox mod S₃.

### Maximal parabolics (= 1-cusp isotropic plane representatives)

Type II rays ℝ≥₀·v (v² = 0) in 𝔉_cox ↔ maximal parabolic subdiagrams of G_cox (disjoint unions of affine Dynkin diagrams Ã_i with Σ|Gᵢ| = 17). The four mod S₃:

1. **Ã₁₇** = nodes [0,1,...,17] (18-cycle)
2. **D̃₁₀Ẽ₇** = [4,5,...,0,1,2,3] ⊔ [18,16,17,0,...] (explicit node sets in paper Fig. 1)
3. **Ẽ₈²Ã₁** = two E₈ components + one A₁
4. **D̃₁₆Ã₁** = nodes [19,5,6,...,0,1,18] ⊔ [3,23]

For each maximal parabolic, the square-zero vectors of its connected components coincide (= the isotropic plane representative J).

### Stabilizer and reflection subgroup

For each 1-cusp J:
- Stab_Γ(J) acts on J ≅ ℤ² via a finite-index subgroup Γ_J ⊂ SL₂(ℤ)
- Boundary component = Γ_J\F_J is a modular curve → j-line A¹_j
- The root sublattice of J^⊥/J defines the Coxeter diagram of the local reflection group

### Tits building recovery

The Tits building of Γ is the simplicial complex with:
- Vertices = Γ-orbits of maximal parabolics (= 1-cusps, 4 of them)
- Edges = incidence of parabolic subgroups (sub-diagram containment in G_cox)
- The building encodes the combinatorial structure of ∂F₂^BB

The face lattice of 𝔉_cox mod O⁺(N) gives the full incidence structure via the order-reversing bijection: faces of 𝔎 ↔ elliptic/maximal parabolic subdiagrams of G_cox.

- [ ] Verify J^⊥/J contains the claimed root sublattice at finite index
- [ ] Compute stabilizer Stab_Γ(J) and its index in SL₂(ℤ)

- [ ] Verify 4 maximal parabolic subdiagrams mod S₃ symmetry
